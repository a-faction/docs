# First setup

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Your server is connected and the panel is empty — that's how everyone starts. Filling it takes about five minutes: describe your community to the AI or follow the steps by hand.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Who:** the server owner or anyone with "Settings access".
    - **Where:** the home page (quick way) or "Site settings" (manual).
    - **Progress:** Site settings → [Overview](../settings/overview.md).

## Quick way: describe your community

While the server has no structure, the home page shows **"Tell us about your community"**.

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. Describe in your own words who you are and how you're organised: departments, positions, who reports to whom.
2. The AI shows a preview of your panel: menu, units, positions. If something's unclear it asks once.
3. **"Change"** — adjust the plan. **"Create server"** — save it. **"Set up manually"** — go to settings.

</div>

This creates the structure, positions, permissions, menu and names. Everything can be edited later.

## Manual way

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. **[Structure](../settings/structure.md)** — units, positions, Discord roles. Without it there's nobody to register.
2. **[Permissions](../settings/access.md)** — who sees what and who can do what.
3. **[Names](../settings/presets.md)** — "Presets → Terms".
4. **Discord** — check in "Overview" that the bot is connected. Set up [bot forms](../discord/discord-forms.md) and [notification channels](../settings/notifications.md).
5. **[Table](../settings/table.md)** — member list columns.
6. **Give yourself a position.** Right after connecting, the owner holds no position. The panel offers **"Assign yourself a position?"** → pick one → **"Assign"**.

</div>

!!! tip "Have another working server?"
    Export its settings to a file and apply them here: **Presets → Settings**. See [Presets and terms](../settings/presets.md).

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The home page is empty | You're the only member so far | Add people — [registration](../work/members.md#register) |
| "No roles yet — nothing to pick" | The structure isn't set up | Add positions in [Structure](../settings/structure.md) |
| "The bot is not on the server" | The bot wasn't added or was removed | [Add the bot](connect-bot.md) |
| "Fill in the structures, positions and roles" | Quick setup has empty fields | Fill in all three blocks |
| "Couldn't get a reply" | The AI didn't respond this time | Try again or set up manually |
| "You already hold a position on this server" | You're already assigned | Refresh the page |
| "This position is no longer in the structure" | The position was deleted | Refresh and pick another |
