# Desktop app

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Like having everything at hand? Install A-Faction as a regular app — its own icon, its own window, updates that install themselves.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Download:** [a-faction.xyz/desktop](https://a-faction.xyz/en/desktop) — the site suggests the right version.
    - **Updates:** automatic.
    - **Sign-in:** with Discord.

## Installing

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. Open [a-faction.xyz/desktop](https://a-faction.xyz/en/desktop) and download the version for your system.
2. Run the downloaded file and follow the installer.
3. Open the app and click "Sign in with Discord".

</div>

## Phone

There's no separate phone app. Open [crm.a-faction.xyz](https://crm.a-faction.xyz) in your browser and choose **"Add to Home Screen"** in the browser menu — the panel will open like an app.

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Windows: "Windows protected your PC" | The build has no paid publisher signature | Click **"More info" → "Run anyway"** |
| macOS: "The app can't be opened" | macOS protection for downloaded apps | Right-click the app → **"Open"** → **"Open"** |
| The app doesn't update | No connection to the update server | Restart the app. Still stuck — download it again |
| White screen | Loading failed | Restart. Check the [status](../help/status.md) |
