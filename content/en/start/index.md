# Getting started

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Welcome! In a couple of minutes your community moves out of spreadsheets and pinned messages into one clear system. Pick your path below.</p>

## I own a community and want to connect it

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. [Connect your server](connect-server.md) on a-faction.xyz — free, about two minutes.
2. [Add the bot](connect-bot.md) to your Discord server — it's one of the steps.
3. [Do the first setup](first-steps.md): structure, permissions, names.
4. Add people — [member registration](../work/members.md#register).

</div>

## I was invited to a server that already runs

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. Ask the leadership to register you, or register through the bot.
2. [Sign in](login.md) at [crm.a-faction.xyz](https://crm.a-faction.xyz) with Discord.
3. See [how the panel is laid out](interface.md), or take the built-in tour.

</div>

## Price

Connecting and every working feature are free, no card needed. Only [Premium](../account/premium.md) is paid — it's profile cosmetics and doesn't affect permissions or features.

## Where it works

| | |
|---|---|
| **Website** [a-faction.xyz](https://a-faction.xyz) | Connecting a new server, feature overview |
| **Panel** [crm.a-faction.xyz](https://crm.a-faction.xyz) | Day-to-day work in a browser, on desktop and phone |
| **App** | Windows and macOS — [details](desktop.md) |
| **Discord bot** | Commands, notifications and requests right in Discord |

The data is shared: an action in Discord shows up in the panel immediately, and vice versa.
