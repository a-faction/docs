# Service status

<p class="af-lead" markdown>The first place to look when something's broken: it shows whether everything on our side is fine right now.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** the "Status" button in the panel footer.
    - **Checks:** every minute.

## What it shows

- website, panel, app downloads, API, database, Discord bot, sending to Discord, Discord itself;
- uptime by day and from different countries;
- response time;
- outage history with how each was fixed.

Times are in your own time zone.

## Something's broken

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. Open the [status](https://status.a-faction.xyz). An outage — we're fixing it.
2. All green — refresh the panel page.
3. Still broken — [support](support.md).

</div>

If an outage affects the panel, a warning bar appears at the top.

!!! tip "Doesn't open from your country?"
    The status page shows availability by country. If yours is red, try a VPN.
