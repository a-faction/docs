# Support

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Real people who help when nothing else did. The reply arrives right in the panel and in your Discord DMs.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** the "Help desk" button in the panel footer.
    - **One open ticket** at a time.
    - **Replies:** in the support panel, the bell and Discord DMs.

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. "Help desk" in the footer.
2. Pick a topic — the start of the text fills in — or just type.
3. Describe what you did, what you expected and what you saw. Attach a screenshot.
4. Send.

</div>

Your nickname, server and position are sent automatically.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Waiting for a reply |
| In progress | We're on it |
| Closed | Solved. Reopen it to reply |

A new reply while the panel is closed: a pop-up and a counter on the button.

## Other ways

- **A-Faction AI** — a button in the support panel.
- `/faction_bug text` in Discord.
- [A-Faction Discord server](https://discord.gg/2cMBp8gjYn).

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "You already have an open ticket" | Write in that one |
| "The ticket is closed. Reopen it before replying" | Reopen the ticket |
| "The message is longer than N characters" | Shorten or split it |
| "Your session expired. Please sign in again" | Sign in again |
| "Could not send the message" | Retry. No connection — write on the Discord server |
