# Privacy

<p class="af-lead" markdown>You decide what to show: age, social links, coins and more can be hidden from others.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** Settings → Privacy.
    - **Server leadership sees everything**, hidden fields included.

## What you can hide

Age · Discord ID · social links · avatar and background · in-game info · coins · achievements · status.

If something is hidden, a crossed-out eye with the list appears in your profile header.

## What's stored

What you and your community enter: nickname, positions, age and social links, the activity log, reports, reprimands, coins, Discord ID and avatar. Details — the [privacy policy](https://a-faction.xyz/en/privacy-policy).

## How the account is protected

- sign-in through Discord only, no passwords;
- everyone sees only their own server's data;
- attempts to bypass permissions are blocked automatically;
- every sign-in is listed in "Sessions".

Details — [Security](https://a-faction.xyz/en/security).

## Deleting your account

There's no button. Leadership removes you from positions; anything else goes through [support](../help/support.md).
