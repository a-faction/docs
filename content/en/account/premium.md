# Premium

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Stand out: a vivid nickname, an avatar frame, animated themes. Premium is purely cosmetic — it doesn't touch permissions.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Duration:** 30 days, ends by itself.
    - **Payment and setup:** Settings → Appearance → Premium block.

## What you get

- 6 themes with animated backgrounds: Sakura, Neon, Rainbow, Gold, Aurora, Galaxy;
- a nickname colour or gradient;
- avatar frame and glow;
- an avatar badge, a title under the nickname;
- profile effects;
- a highlighted table row;
- a project supporter badge.

Others can see all of it.

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Paid but no Premium | Activation hasn't happened yet | Follow the instructions in the Premium block. Still nothing — [support](../help/support.md) with proof of payment |
| "Premium is not active right now" | It expired | Renew |
| Signed out or blocked after changing cosmetics | A protection glitch | Contact [support](../help/support.md) — it's not your fault |
