# Roster export

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Need to post your leadership list on a forum? The panel builds it for you with current nicknames and terms — just copy and paste.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** side menu → "Data export".

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. **"Edit"** — opens the template with a live preview.
2. Insert blocks: by unit, by person, or as a list. Filter by unit and position.
3. Set text for empty positions (e.g. "Vacant").
4. **"Done"** → **"Copy"** and paste it wherever you need.

</div>

- Blocks fill in current data: nickname, position, term.
- **"Refresh"** pulls in fresh data.
- The template saves itself.
- The markup is BB-code, so forums display it properly.

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The data is stale | The preview wasn't refreshed | Click "Refresh" |
| The forum shows `[b]` tags | The forum doesn't support BB-code | Paste in BB-code mode or use another forum |
