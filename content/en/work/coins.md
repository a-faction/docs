# Coins

<p class="af-lead" markdown>An internal currency that motivates: coins are earned for work and spent on whatever you come up with — perks, roles, bonuses.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Ask for coins:** "Request coins".
    - **Spend:** "Spend coins".
    - **Give and approve:** the "Coins" permission (Add/deduct).
    - **Discord:** `/coins @member`, `/shop @member`.

## Where coins come from

- an approved coin request;
- an approved [report](reports.md);
- a leader adding them manually in the [profile](profile.md#actions) or with `/coins`.

## Request

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. "Request coins".
2. One request: nickname, amount, reason. Or a list, one line per person: `Nickname Amount Reason`.
3. Submit. A leader with the permission decides.

</div>

## Spend { #spend }

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. "Spend coins" → pick an item and quantity.
2. Submit the purchase request.
3. A leader approves — the coins are deducted.

</div>

Items and prices are managed by leaders with the permission.

## Rules

- The balance is kept per server and per unit.
- Fractions are possible; the math is exact.
- Every movement is logged: who asked, who approved, how much, balance before and after.
- A rejected request doesn't change the balance.
- Requests can be decided in bulk, even if the author was already removed.
- You can't give coins to yourself — only to people [below you](../settings/hierarchy.md).

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not enough coins in the faction (you have N, need M) | Too few coins | Save up or pick something cheaper |
| "You have no factions on this server" | No position | Switch server |
| "Player not found" | Wrong nickname or the person was removed | Check the nickname |
| "Errors found: N" | The list has bad lines | Fix the highlighted lines |
| "The item was not found" | The item was deleted | Refresh the page |
| No "Approve" button | No "Coins" permission | [Permissions](../settings/access.md) |
| "This action cannot be applied to yourself" | Not allowed on yourself | Ask another leader |
