# Planner

<p class="af-lead" markdown>A schedule nobody can double-book: members claim free time themselves, and the system never lets two people take the same slot.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** side menu → "Planner".
    - **Time:** UTC+3, the same for every member.
    - **Settings:** Site settings → [Planner](../settings/planner.md).

## Take a slot

Click a free cell. The slot is taken right away or after a confirmation window — depending on the settings. The cell then shows the unit, position or nickname.

## Rules

- a taken slot can't be taken again;
- past time only if allowed;
- neighbouring slots are blocked for a set number of minutes;
- the person who took it, or leadership, can cancel;
- other people's changes show instantly.

## Past days

Day arrows and a calendar (days with bookings have a dot). Past days are view-only.

## A new day

The grid clears itself at midnight panel time (UTC+3). Past days stay in the history.

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The time "doesn't match" mine | The panel uses UTC+3 for everyone | Account for your time-zone difference |
| "Could not take the slot" | Someone just took it, or a neighbour blocks it | Pick another |
| Can't take past time | Disabled in settings | — |
| "The settings do not say what to show when a slot is taken" | Not configured | Admins: [Planner](../settings/planner.md) → "What to provide" |
| "No faction data" / no position data | You hold no position on this server | Switch server |
| "No slots were booked on this day" | Empty day | Pick a day with a dot in the calendar |
| "Could not cancel the slot" | It's someone else's | The owner or leadership cancels |
