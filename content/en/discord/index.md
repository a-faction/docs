# Discord

<p class="af-lead" markdown>The A-Faction bot lives right on your Discord server and does everything the panel does — without leaving Discord.</p>

| What the bot does | Article |
|---|---|
| Assigns and removes roles at registration and removal | [Members](../work/members.md) |
| Answers commands: profile, reprimands, coins, log | [Bot commands](commands.md) |
| Request buttons, auto-replies, menus, role commands | [Discord forms](discord-forms.md) |
| Posts announcements in its own name | [Bot messages](webhooks.md) |
| Reprimand appeals in forum threads | [Reprimand appeals](../work/appeals.md#discord) |
| Notifications to channels and DMs | [Notifications](../settings/notifications.md) |
| Records interviews | [Interviews](../work/interviews.md) |
| Form answer cards with decision buttons | [Forms](../work/forms.md) |

## When Discord is down

The action in the panel still goes through, and you get a warning "The action is done, but the notification was not sent…". No data is lost.

How to add the bot and give it permissions — [Add the bot](../start/connect-bot.md).

!!! note "Bot language"
    The bot replies in Discord in Russian. In these docs we quote its messages in the original with an English translation in brackets.
