# A-Faction Docs

Everything that usually lives in spreadsheets, pinned messages and someone's memory — in one system: roster, positions, reprimands, leaves, reports, coins and interviews. Here's how it all works, where to start, and what to do when something doesn't.

## Where to start { #start }

<div class="af-paths" markdown>

<div class="af-path" markdown>

<span class="af-path__tag">Community owner</span>

### I'm connecting my server

Free, about five minutes including setup.

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. [Connect your server](start/connect-server.md) on a-faction.xyz and add the bot.
2. [Set up the structure](start/first-steps.md) — or just describe your community to the AI.
3. [Add people](work/members.md#register) — the bot hands out Discord roles itself.

</div>

</div>

<div class="af-path" markdown>

<span class="af-path__tag">Member</span>

### I was added to a server

Leadership or the bot registers you — no password needed.

<div class="af-steps" markdown>

1. [Sign in](start/login.md) at crm.a-faction.xyz with Discord.
2. See [how the panel is laid out](start/interface.md), or take the built-in tour.
3. Submit your first [report](work/reports.md) or [leave](work/inactive.md) when you need to.

</div>

## When something doesn't work { #fixes }

<div class="af-fixes" markdown>

<div class="af-fix" markdown>
<span class="af-fix__what">"Access denied" when signing in</span>
<span class="af-fix__how">Your Discord account isn't on the roster yet. Ask your leadership to register you. [Details](start/login.md#troubleshooting)</span>
</div>

<div class="af-fix" markdown>
<span class="af-fix__what">A section or button is missing</span>
<span class="af-fix__how">It's hidden for your position, or your position lacks that permission. [Details](settings/access.md#troubleshooting)</span>
</div>

<div class="af-fix" markdown>
<span class="af-fix__what">The bot didn't give a Discord role</span>
<span class="af-fix__how">The bot's role sits below the role it's assigning — move it up in Discord's role settings. [Details](start/connect-bot.md#troubleshooting)</span>
</div>

<div class="af-fix" markdown>
<span class="af-fix__what">The confirmation code doesn't arrive</span>
<span class="af-fix__how">The bot can only DM people it shares a server with. Join the A-Faction Discord and open your DMs. [Details](start/connect-server.md#troubleshooting)</span>
</div>

</div>
