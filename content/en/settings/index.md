# Server settings

<p class="af-lead" markdown>This is where you decide how your server works: structure, permissions, discipline, notifications. Nothing changes until you press "Save".</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Who:** the owner and anyone with "Settings access".
    - **Where:** side menu → "Site settings".

## Saving

Edits pile up across every section. A section with edits has a dot; a bar appears at the bottom: **"Careful — you have unsaved changes!"** with **"Reset"** and **"Save changes"**. Nothing changes until you press it.

On a phone you see the section list first; inside a section there's an **"All settings"** button.

Each section has a **"How it works"** block with an animated hint.

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No "Site settings" item | No "Settings access", or it's limited to some positions | Ask the owner |
| "Could not save the changes" | Failure or lost connection | Stay on the page and press again |
| Data didn't load | Failure | Refresh the page |
| Left the page — edits are gone | "Save changes" wasn't pressed | Always save before leaving |
