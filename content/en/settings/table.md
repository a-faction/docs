# Member table

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Decide what leaders see in the roster: which columns, what they're called, and in what order.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** Site settings → Table.

- The switch turns a column on or off.
- **"Rename"** — your own column name.
- Drag to reorder.

By default: nickname, Discord ID, unit, reprimands. Warnings, ministry reprimands and immunity always sit inside the "Reprimands" column.

Row density is a personal choice — [personal settings](../account/settings.md).

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "No enabled columns" | Everything is off | Turn at least one on |
| A column is missing | It's off | Turn it on |
| Changes don't show | Not saved | "Save changes" at the bottom |
