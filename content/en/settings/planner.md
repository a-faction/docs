# Planner settings

<p class="af-lead" markdown>Fit the planner to your rhythm: hours, slot length and what a click does.</p>

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **Where:** Site settings → Planner.
    - **Time:** UTC+3 for everyone.

| Setting | What it sets |
|---|---|
| Day start and end | Grid hours |
| Step | Slot length |
| Neighbour slot blocking | How many minutes around a taken slot are closed |
| Past time | Whether past slots can be taken |
| Click action | Take right away or open a confirmation window |
| What to provide | Unit, position or nickname in the slot |
| Window text | What the confirmation says |

How to use it — [Planner](../work/planner.md).

## Troubleshooting { #troubleshooting }

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| People see "The settings do not say what to show" | "What to provide" is empty | Pick at least one |
| Members don't see the section | It's hidden | [Permissions](access.md) → visibility |
